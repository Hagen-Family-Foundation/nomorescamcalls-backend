# NoMoreScamCalls Beta Participant Agreement

## Welcome

Thank you for participating in the NoMoreScamCalls Beta Program.

Your feedback will help us improve the product before its public release. We appreciate your time and your willingness to help.

Your honest feedback—good or bad—is one of the most valuable parts of this beta program.

## About the Beta

NoMoreScamCalls is still under development.

While we work hard to provide a reliable service, you may experience occasional bugs, unexpected behavior, feature changes, or temporary service interruptions. That's a normal part of beta testing.

Although we strive to provide a reliable service, we cannot guarantee that every call will be handled perfectly or that the service will always be available.

## Your Participation

By joining this beta program, you agree to:

- Use the service for normal, everyday phone calls.
- Report problems or suggestions when you can.
- Keep information about the beta and the product confidential unless we give you permission to share it.
- Avoid intentionally misusing or interfering with the service.

## Your Privacy

We use your information only to operate, support, and improve the NoMoreScamCalls service during the beta program.

This may include your account information, call-related information, system logs, and any feedback you choose to provide.

## Ending Your Participation

You may leave the beta program at any time.

We may also end your participation if necessary for operational, security, or program-related reasons.

## Ownership

Your participation in this beta program does not give you ownership of NoMoreScamCalls or any financial interest in the product or company.

## Acceptance

By selecting **I Accept**, you confirm that:

- You understand this is a beta program.
- You understand the service may change during testing.
- You are participating voluntarily.
- You agree to keep the beta confidential.
- You understand that participating does not create any ownership or financial interest in NoMoreScamCalls.

Thank you for helping us build a better way to protect people from unwanted and scam phone calls.
