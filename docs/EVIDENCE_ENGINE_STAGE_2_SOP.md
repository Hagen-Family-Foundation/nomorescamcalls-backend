# Evidence Engine Stage 2 SOP

## Trigger

Stage 2 begins when it receives the completed Stage 1 box.

## Responsibility

Stage 2 owns the full caller-response process.

It:

- plays Request #1,
- captures and evaluates Response #1,
- plays Request #2 only when Response #1 is not satisfactory,
- captures and evaluates Response #2,
- applies Stage 2 deductions,
- conditionally performs the IPQS lookup,
- applies any IPQS deduction,
- produces the completed Stage 2 box.

## Request #1

“Our apologies. Please speak clearly with your name and reason for calling so we may route your call to the correct department.”

## Response #1 Evaluation

Response #1 is evaluated immediately.

Stage 2 determines:

- whether a usable name was provided,
- whether a usable reason for calling was provided.

If both are satisfactory, Request #2 is not played.

If either is unsatisfactory, Request #2 is required.

## Request #2

“Again, please speak slowly and clearly, stating your name and reason for calling so that we may connect you promptly.”

## Response #2 Evaluation

Response #2 is evaluated independently.

Response #1 and Response #2 are not combined or compared.

Response #2 exists because Response #1 did not satisfy the request.

## Stage 2 Deductions

After the required response cycle is complete:

- no usable name: 15-point deduction,
- no usable reason: 15-point deduction,
- usable name: no deduction,
- usable reason: no deduction.

The reason a response was unusable does not affect scoring.

## IPQS Rule

After Stage 2 deductions are established:

- standing 86–100: no IPQS lookup,
- standing 76–85: perform IPQS lookup,
- standing 75 or below: no IPQS lookup.

A derogatory IPQS result applies a 10-point deduction according to the IPQS rule set.

## Research Evidence

Stage 2 preserves all useful caller-interaction information, including:

- transcripts,
- audio references,
- response timing,
- detected language,
- voice characteristics,
- environmental characteristics,
- other measurable call observations.

Research evidence never affects live scoring.

## Output Box

The completed Stage 2 box contains all information accumulated during the call, including:

- Stage 1 information,
- both requests,
- captured responses,
- extracted name and reason,
- acceptance results,
- Stage 2 deductions,
- IPQS findings when performed,
- IPQS deduction when applicable,
- research evidence,
- accumulated deduction record.

Stage 2 completes all work within its responsibility before producing the box.
